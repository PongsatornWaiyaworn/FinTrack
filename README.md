# FinTrack

**FinTrack** is an expense tracking platform designed to help you understand and manage your finances with clarity and confidence.

> Track your expenses.  
> Gain clarity over your finances.

---

## About FinTrack

FinTrack is built for **everyday life, office work, and small teams**.  
It helps you easily monitor spending, analyze financial behavior, and understand where your money goes — all in one **simple and secure system**.

Whether you're managing personal expenses or tracking shared costs, FinTrack keeps everything organized and easy to follow.

- Track and categorize expenses
- Clear financial overview
- Built with security in mind
